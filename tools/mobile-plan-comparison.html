Mobile Plan Data Comparison

Compare two mobile plans by cost per GB and check which one fits your monthly use.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Prices and allowances vary by provider and country. Enter the plans you are comparing.

How this calculator works

Cost per GB is the monthly price divided by the data allowance. A plan fits if its allowance is at least as large as your monthly use.

Worked example

Plan A at 30 for 50 GB costs 0.60 per GB and does not fit 100 GB of use. Plan B at 45 for 150 GB costs 0.30 per GB and fits 100 GB of use.
