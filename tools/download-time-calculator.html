Download Time Calculator

Work out how long a download will take at your connection speed.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Real speeds vary; this is the time at the speed you enter.

How this calculator works

A gigabyte is 8,000 megabits. Divide the file size in megabits by the speed in megabits per second to get the time in seconds.

Worked example

A 5 GB file is 40,000 megabits. At 50 Mbps it takes 800 seconds, or about 13.3 minutes.
