Internet Data Usage Calculator

Estimate your monthly data use from streaming, browsing, calls and gaming, using rates you can adjust.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Data rates are examples and vary by quality, app and settings. Enter the rate that matches your quality.

How this calculator works

Monthly use is daily use multiplied by 30. Daily use is the sum of each activity's hours multiplied by its data rate. Browsing is estimated at 0.2 GB per hour and video calls at 1.5 GB per hour in this tool; change the streaming rate to match your quality.

Worked example

Streaming 2 hours a day at 3 GB per hour, browsing 3 hours, and 1 hour of video calls a day uses about 8.1 GB a day, or 243 GB a month.
