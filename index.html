Frequently asked questions

How much data does streaming use?

It depends on quality. Standard quality uses less than HD, and HD uses less than 4K. Enter your quality's rate in the calculator.

How do I estimate my monthly data use?

Add the time for each activity, multiply by its data rate, and multiply by 30 days. The estimator does this for you.

How long will a download take?

Divide the file size in megabits by your speed in Mbps. A 5 GB file at 50 Mbps takes about 13 minutes.

What is the difference between GB and Mbps?

GB is a size, Mbps is a speed. A file size in GB divided by a speed in Mbps, after converting GB to megabits, gives the time.

How much data does a video call use?

It depends on the app and quality, but a common example rate is about 1.5 GB per hour for video, far less for audio only.

How do I choose a mobile plan?

Work out your real monthly use first, then choose a plan whose allowance covers it, and compare cost per GB.

Does music streaming use much data?

Less than video. At an example rate of 0.15 GB per hour, two hours a day is about 9 GB a month.

How can I reduce my data use?

Lower streaming quality, download on Wi-Fi, and turn off video autoplay on mobile data.
